DEVELOPMENT HISTORY

Here you can see the whole development timeline for the mod!

2021

October 7
Some concept art was made, along with a Week 4 track being made. The mod at this time was not a final decision and was made for fun.
October 19
The first version of "Ständchen" was created.
October 21
The first version of "Closed Heart" (bgm02c) was created. Though, this track was not intended for the mod at this time.
November 2
A storyline for was made. It would have five weeks centered around the idea of depression, and visualizing it with inspiration from We've Destroyed All The Hope In This World.
November 7
The first version of "Dissociation" was created, which at the time was titled "Corrosion".
December 16
The first version of "Reminiscence" was created.
December 17
The first version of "Somberness" was created, which at the time was titled "Dejected".

2022

January 1
The first version of "It's Destroyed" was created.
January 24
The first version of "Éclat Noirci" (bgm02a) was created. Though, this song was being used for another project at the time.
January 25
"Accumulation" was created, which was unnamed at the time.
February 7
A discord server for the mod was created, officially making this mod a reality.
February 25
The first version of "Rupture" was created.
February 28
"Organ Donor" was created.
March 13
"Ständchen," "Dissociation," "It's Destroyed", "Reminiscence", and the future track mentioned earlier all get remastered for the first time. "Dissociation" was still titled "Corrosion" at this time.
April 23
The first versions of "You Can't Run (cover)" and "Porcelain Smile (Pause Theme)" were created.
April 24
"Vibrancy" was created.
May 5
The first version of "Unseen" was created, which was unnamed at the time.
May 19
"Somberness" was remastered for the first time, which was still titled "Dejected".
June 2
"You Can't Run (cover)" was remastered for the first time.
June 11
Windo's Week 1 sprites were finished.
June 12
"Rupture" was remastered for the first time.
June 21
The first demo build was made.
July 5
"Somberness" was remastered for the second time, which was still titled "Dejected".
July 14
"It's Destroyed" was remastered for the second time.
July 16
The banner and menu background was made.
July 17
The second demo build was made.
August 21
Dialogue portraits for Windo were created.
September 5
The first version of "Oh My Lordy" (bgm01a) was created.
September 19
The second and last version of "Dissociation" was made.
October 2
The second and last version of "Reminiscence" was made, along with the third demo build being made.
November 2
The first and last version of "Oh My Lordy" (bgm01a) was created.
November 11
The banner for the Version 2.x update was created.
November 15
The Week 3 rendition of Organ Donor ("Organ Eater") was created.

2023

February 11
"Rupture" was remastered for the second time.
February 20
"Closed Heart" (bgm02c) was remastered for the first and last time.
March 10
The official demo of the mod was released on GameBanana as Demo 1.00, along with its official soundtrack.
June 7
"Ständchen" was remastered for the second and last time.
June 16
"It's Destroyed" was remastered for the third time.
July 28
The first build for Version 2.x was made.
October 8
The first version of "Who I Was" was created.
October 12
"Unseen" was remastered for the first and last time.
October 15
"Porcelain Smile (Pause Theme)" was remastered for the first and last time.
December 8
The second build for Version 2.x was made.

2024

May 3
The third build for Version 2.x was made.
May 11
The first update to the demo was released as Demo 1.01.
May 20
The second update to the demo was released as Demo 1.02.
May 24
"It's Destroyed" was remastered for the fourth and final time.
May 25
Week 2's background assets were made.
May 28
The game over music (Closed Field) was created.
June 5
The fourth build for Version 2.x was made.
June 6
"Rupture" was remastered for the third and final time.
June 19
The third update to the demo was released as Demo 1.03.
June 25
The first version of "Dangerous Zone" (bgm03a) was created.
June 28
"Éclat Noirci" (bgm02a) was remastered for the first time.
July 2
Week 3's background assets were made.
September 18
Windo's Week 3 sprites were created. The same day "Casino Park" was created, but was not made for the mod at this time.
September 30
The fifth build for Version 2.x was made.
October 1
"Step Up!" (bgm01b) and "Nightfall" (bgm02b) were created.
October 3
"Squ're" (bgm01d) was created.
October 6
"FASTER!" (bgm01c) was created. Later that day, the fourth and last update to the demo was released as Demo 1.04.
October 7
"Nullkeeper" (bgm03c) was created.
October 21
"Without Care, Sky-High" (bgm02d) was created.
October 27
"Éclat Noirci" (bgm02a) is remastered for the second and last time. "Dangerous Zone" (bgm03a) is remastered for the first and last time. "Riptide Burnout" (bgm03b) is created.
October 30
"It's Destroyed" background assets were made.
November 25
The sixth and final build for Version 2.x was made.
November 27
The first major release of the mod was released on Gamebanana and Gamejolt as Version 2.03.
November 30
"Who I Was" gets remastered for the first and last time.

2025

February 4
"You Can't Run (cover)" gets remastered for the second and last time.
February 12
The first build for Version 2.1 is made public for everyone to try.
July 8
"It's Destroyed" background assets were redone.
July 22
Assets for "Casino Park" are made.
December 20
Windo's computer gets wiped, leading to the mod's efforts being set back by a few months.

2026

March 11
bgm03d is created.
